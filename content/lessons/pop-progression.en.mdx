Four chords, thousands of songs: *Let It Be*, *No Woman, No Cry*, *With or Without
You* and countless others sit on **I – V – vi – IV**. The trick is the vi: it is the
**relative minor** of I and shares two notes with it. That's why the progression sounds
familiar and a little wistful at the same time.

## Play it like a pianist

Use what you learned in the last lesson: short moves. From C to G/B, to Am/C, to F/C.
The right hand stays almost in one place – and that is exactly how pianists accompany
pop songs.
