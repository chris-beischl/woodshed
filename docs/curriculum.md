# woodshed — Curriculum

Goal: **solo jazz piano** in the bebop and smooth-jazz idiom, fluent in all 12 keys.
Lesson content is German (jazz terms stay English); this planning document is English.

## Principles

1. **No information in a vacuum.** After its introduction a chord only appears inside a
   progression, a lick or a tune. Every lesson answers: where does this occur, what does
   it do, what does it sound like?
2. **Every exercise has a playful hook** (see "Fun mechanics"). Drill is the vehicle,
   not the point.
3. **Each unit ends in music**: a tune's changes and a lick played over them.
4. **Keys roll out gradually**: new material starts in C, F, Bb, Eb; further keys unlock
   as earlier ones stabilise. Never 36 new cards in one day.
5. **Two hands from Unit 3 on**: left hand comps (shells → rootless), right hand plays
   lines. That is what solo piano is.
6. **Only changes and generic licks.** Melodies and transcribed solos are copyrighted;
   chord progressions and idiomatic vocabulary are not. Tunes are practised from their
   changes.

## Fun mechanics

| Mechanic | Used for | What makes it fun |
|---|---|---|
| **Call & response** | licks | the app plays the lick, you answer it — like trading with a teacher |
| **Tempo ladder** | licks, progressions | each clean run raises the tempo; personal best per lick |
| **Circle of keys** | all cards | the circle-of-fourths map fills up as you master a lick or chord in each key |
| **Backing groove** | progressions, tunes, licks | bass + drums loop under you (walking for bebop, Rhodes groove for smooth) |
| **Lick drop** | licks over tunes | the groove plays a tune's changes; you drop your lick in at the right bar |
| **Streaks & session map** | daily practice | already built: streak counter, end-of-session key map |
| **Tune unlock** | units | finishing a unit unlocks its tune as a playable "gig" |
